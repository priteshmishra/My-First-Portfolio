## 1. Crystallographic Macromolecular Structures (PDB)

All molecular structure coordinates are derived from the Worldwide Protein Data Bank (wwPDB) and are publicly accessible under open scientific access terms:

1. **B-DNA Dodecamer (Dickerson-Drew Double Helix)**
   - **PDB ID:** `1BNA`
   - **Classification:** Double-stranded B-DNA oligonucleotide (`CGCGAATTCGCG`)
   - **Resolution:** 1.90 Å (X-Ray Diffraction)
   - **Citation:** Drew, H. R., & Dickerson, R. E. (1981). *Structure of a B-DNA dodecamer: conformation and dynamics.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 78(4), 2179–2183.
   - **DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.78.4.2179](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.78.4.2179)
   - **Repository:** [wwPDB Entry 1BNA](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1BNA)

2. **Crambin Seed Storage Protein**
   - **PDB ID:** `1CRN`
   - **Classification:** Plant seed storage protein (*Crambe abyssinica*)
   - **Resolution:** 1.50 Å (X-Ray Diffraction)
   - **Citation:** Teeter, M. M. (1984). *Water structure of a hydrophobic protein at atomic resolution: Pentagonal rings of water molecules in crystals of crambin.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 81(19), 6014–6018.
   - **DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.81.19.6014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.81.19.6014)
   - **Repository:** [wwPDB Entry 1CRN](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1CRN)

3. **Caffeine (1,3,7-Trimethylxanthine)**
   - **Formula:** C8H10N4O2
   - **Classification:** Small molecule purine alkaloid
   - **Citation:** *The Merck Index: An Encyclopedia of Chemicals, Drugs, and Biologicals* (15th Edition). Royal Society of Chemistry (2013).

## 2. Scientific Literature Citations

The educational research notes in the platform cite the following landmark peer-reviewed publications:

1. **The Molecular Structure of Nucleic Acids**
   - Watson, J. D., & Crick, F. H. (1953). *Molecular structure of nucleic acids: a structure for deoxyribose nucleic acid.* Nature, 171(4356), 737–738. [DOI: 10.1038/171737a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/171737a0)

2. **The Central Dogma of Molecular Biology**
   - Crick, F. (1970). *Central dogma of molecular biology.* Nature, 227(5258), 561–563. [DOI: 10.1038/227561a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/227561a0)

3. **Global Dynamic Programming Sequence Alignment**
   - Needleman, S. B., & Wunsch, C. D. (1970). *A general method applicable to the search for similarities in the amino acid sequence of two proteins.* Journal of Molecular Biology, 48(3), 443–453. [DOI: 10.1016/0022-2836(70)90057-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2836(70)90057-4)

4. **Local Dynamic Programming Sequence Alignment**
   - Smith, T. F., & Waterman, M. S. (1981). *Identification of common molecular subsequences.* Journal of Molecular Biology, 147(1), 195–197. [DOI: 10.1016/0022-2836(81)90087-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2836(81)90087-5)

5. **Heuristic Database Sequence Searching (BLAST)**
   - Altschul, S. F., Gish, W., Miller, W., Myers, E. W., & Lipman, D. J. (1990). *Basic local alignment search tool.* Journal of Molecular Biology, 215(3), 403–410. [DOI: 10.1016/S0022-2836(05)80360-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-2836(05)80360-2)

6. **The Protein Data Bank Foundation**
   - Berman, H. M., Westbrook, J., Feng, Z., Gilliland, G., Bhat, T. N., Weissig, H., Shindyalov, I. N., & Bourne, P. E. (2000). *The Protein Data Bank.* Nucleic Acids Research, 28(1), 235–242. [DOI: 10.1093/nar/28.1.235](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/28.1.235)
